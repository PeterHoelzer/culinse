# Pricing — Culinse (culinse.com)

Last updated: 2026-11-25. Culinse is free. There is no paid plan, no subscription and nothing to buy.

## What is included (free)
- Full access to the in-house Culinse recipe corpus (AI-created, editorially reviewed) with search & filters (diet, cooking time, cuisine)
- Save recipes (favorites), no limit
- Recipe collections, no limit
- Weekly meal planner (breakfast, lunch & dinner for the whole week, several plans) with automatic shopping list (summed quantities, sorted by category, estimated € total at German discounter prices)
- Your own recipes, including import from a recipe link
- Share meal plans & collections via link
- Free grocery list calculator: https://culinse.com/en/grocery-list-calculator
- Free 7-day meal-prep plan (PDF) via newsletter signup

## Account
- Browsing, searching and reading recipes work without an account.
- Saving recipes and using collections, the weekly planner, the shopping list and your own recipes require a free account.
- Signup: https://culinse.com/en/login (free, email-based)

## Notes for agents
- There is no checkout, no trial and no upgrade page.
- The former "Pro" subscription is no longer offered. Its old address https://culinse.com/en/pro (German: https://culinse.com/de/pro) redirects to the weekly meal planner page.
